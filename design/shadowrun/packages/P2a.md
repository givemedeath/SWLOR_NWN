# P2a — Barrens district design

**Status:** design complete; validate against the first finished street
**Source of truth:** [../districts/barrens.md](../districts/barrens.md)

## Deliverable

Define the first Erie district’s geography, routes, factions, services, NPC delivery points, encounter
roles, and run hooks before multiplying areas. Names and relationships live in the district document
so setting vocabulary can be audited or renamed without searching production content.

The district concept may span 30–50 areas, but only a 5–8 area proof street is authorized initially:

1. arrival connection / street hub;
2. fixer’s bar;
3. cyberclinic;
4. gang turf encounter;
5. residential/squat route;
6. district edge or dock route.

The layout must support the Phase 3 loop, not merely sightseeing: Johnson and contract delivery,
legwork, hostile route, extraction/return route, payout, recovery, and repeat access. Map these
delivery points together before area generation.

## Gate

Review the paper route against [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md)’s onboarding, party lifecycle, economy, and run
requirements. After P2b’s first exterior is finished, update the district standards with measured
lighting, fog, prop density, navigation, transition, download, and frame-time results.

If that one street does not communicate the intended world without explanation, stop expansion and
reconsider the art direction or platform.
